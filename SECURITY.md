# Security policy

Pesa Clarity handles people's financial messages, so we take reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately through GitHub: open the affected repository, go to **Security > Report a vulnerability**. Do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact you expect. We will acknowledge within 3 working days and keep you updated until it is fixed.

## Scope

- The Android app, its on-device database, backups and SMS handling
- Parser packs and their signing
- The sign-in, backup vault and federated learning server
- Model release and privacy audit tooling

Out of scope: denial of service, social engineering, and reports from automated scanners without a working proof.

## Supported versions

Pre-alpha: only the latest `main` of each repository is supported.

## Disclosure

We ask for 90 days, or until a fix ships, before public disclosure, and we credit reporters who want credit.
