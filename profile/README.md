# Pesa Clarity

Your money, made clear, without giving your data away.

Pesa Clarity is a free, ad-free Android app that turns M-Pesa and bank messages into a clear daily plan: what you can spend today, what is due, where money leaks, and when you will be debt-free. All analysis runs on the phone. The server only signs you in, holds backups it cannot read, and combines anonymous model updates.

Everything is open source, so anyone can check that money data never leaves the phone.

## Principles

1. **Free forever, no ads.**
2. **Your data stays yours, on your phone.**
3. **Plain answers, not spreadsheets.**
4. **Export in two taps.**
5. **Honest money maths.**
